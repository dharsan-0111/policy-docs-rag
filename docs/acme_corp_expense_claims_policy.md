# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Claims Policy

This document outlines the rules, limits, and submission procedures for incurring and claiming business-related expenses at Acme Corp. All expense claims are processed through our central platform, the **AcmeSpend Tracker**.

## 1. Acceptable Expenses and Limits

Acme Corp reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary, and actual expenses incurred while conducting company business. 

### 1.1 Meals and Entertainment
*   **Daily Limit:** The maximum allowable expense for personal meals while traveling on company business is **$75.00 per day** (inclusive of taxes and tips).
*   **Receipt Requirements:** Any single meal expense exceeding **$25.00** requires an itemized receipt. A credit card terminal slip showing only the total amount is *not* sufficient.
*   **Alcohol:** Acme Corp does not reimburse the cost of alcoholic beverages during standard business travel meals. Alcohol is only permitted for approved client entertainment events (Expense Code 5520), capped at two drinks per attendee.

### 1.2 Flights and Air Travel
All flights must be booked through the Acme Travel Portal (Concur integrated).
*   **Duration < 6 Hours:** Economy class is strictly mandatory.
*   **Duration > 6 Hours:** Employees may book Premium Economy.
*   **Business Class:** Only C-Suite executives (VP and above) are permitted to book Business Class, regardless of flight duration.
*   **Baggage:** Acme Corp will reimburse the cost of one standard checked bag.

### 1.3 Accommodation
Hotel limits vary based on the destination city tier.
*   **Tier 1 Cities (e.g., New York, London, San Francisco, Tokyo):** Maximum **$250.00 per night** (excluding taxes).
*   **Tier 2 and Other Cities:** Maximum **$150.00 per night** (excluding taxes).
Mini-bar charges, in-room movies, and personal laundry (for trips under 5 days) are non-reimbursable.

### 1.4 International Travel Per Diem
For international travel lasting more than 3 consecutive days, employees may opt for a fixed Per Diem instead of submitting individual meal receipts.
*   **Per Diem Rate:** **$110.00 USD per day**. 
*   If you claim the Per Diem, you cannot submit any additional receipts for meals, snacks, or local incidental transport (like short bus rides).

## 2. The AcmeSpend Tracker System

All expense claims must be submitted digitally. Paper claims are no longer accepted.

### 2.1 Step-by-Step Submission Guide
1.  **Capture:** Use the AcmeSpend mobile app to take a clear photo of your itemized receipt immediately after purchase.
2.  **Login:** Access the web portal at `spend.acme.corp` or use the mobile app.
3.  **Create Report:** Click "+ New Expense Report". Name the report clearly (e.g., "Q3 Client Onsite - Chicago").
4.  **Add Expenses:** Upload your receipt images. The system's OCR will attempt to read the merchant, date, and amount. *You must manually verify these fields.*
5.  **Categorization (CRITICAL):** You must select the correct 4-digit accounting code for every line item. Common codes include:
    *   **5510:** Travel - Airfare
    *   **5511:** Travel - Hotel
    *   **5515:** Travel - Meals (Self)
    *   **5520:** Client Entertainment
    *   **6030:** Office Supplies (Remote Workers)
6.  **Submit:** Once all expenses for the trip/month are added, click "Submit to Manager".

### 2.2 Corporate Credit Cards
Employees who travel more than 4 times a year are eligible for an Acme Corp Corporate Card (issued by GreenBank). 
*   Charges made on the corporate card automatically sync to the AcmeSpend Tracker within 48 hours.
*   You must still attach receipts and categorize these synced transactions.
*   Personal expenses accidentally charged to the corporate card must be flagged as "Personal" in AcmeSpend, and the amount will be deducted from your next paycheck.

## 3. Deadlines and Processing Times

Strict adherence to submission deadlines is required for accounting compliance.

*   **Submission Deadline:** All expenses must be submitted in the AcmeSpend Tracker within **30 calendar days** of the transaction date. 
*   **Late Submissions:** Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days require a written justification and VP-level approval. Claims older than 60 days will be automatically rejected and will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.
*   **Manager Approval SLA:** Line managers are required to review and approve/reject expense reports within **5 business days** of submission.
*   **Payout Schedule:** Approved reimbursements are processed twice a month. 
    *   Reports fully approved by the 10th are paid on the **15th**.
    *   Reports fully approved by the 25th are paid on the **30th**.
    Reimbursements are deposited directly into the bank account on file for payroll.

## 4. Audits and Compliance
The Finance Department conducts random audits on 15% of all submitted expense reports. If a report is flagged for audit, processing may be delayed by up to 5 additional business days. Fraudulent claims, including duplicated receipts or altered amounts, constitute gross misconduct and will lead to immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

For assistance with the AcmeSpend system, contact `finance-support@acme.corp`.
