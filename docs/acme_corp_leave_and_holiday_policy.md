# Acme Corp Global Leave and Holiday Policy

Welcome to the comprehensive guide on Acme Corp's Leave and Holiday policies. We believe that rest, recovery, and personal time are essential for a healthy, productive workforce. This document outlines the specifics of our leave accrual, holiday schedules, and the procedures for requesting time off using the AcmeLeave Portal.

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)

Acme Corp provides generous Paid Time Off (PTO) to ensure all employees have adequate time for vacation and personal matters.

### 1.1 Accrual Rates and Limits
All full-time employees accrue PTO based on their tenure with Acme Corp. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis.
*   **0-3 Years of Service:** 24 days per calendar year (accrued at exactly 2.0 days per month).
*   **4-7 Years of Service:** 28 days per calendar year (accrued at exactly 2.33 days per month).
*   **8+ Years of Service:** 32 days per calendar year (accrued at exactly 2.66 days per month).

Accruals occur on the 15th of every month. The maximum PTO balance any employee can hold at a given time is 40 days. Once the 40-day cap is reached, accruals will pause until the balance is reduced below the cap.

### 1.2 Carryover Policy
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 days of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any days beyond the 5-day limit remaining on December 31st at 11:59 PM EST will be automatically forfeited. There is no cash-out option for unused PTO at the end of the year, except where mandated by local law upon termination.

### 1.3 Requesting PTO
All PTO requests must be submitted through the **AcmeLeave Portal** (accessible at `leave.acme.corp`). 
*   **Short Notice (1-2 days):** Must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance.
*   **Standard Leave (3-9 days):** Must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance.
*   **Extended Leave (10+ days):** Must be submitted at least 30 calendar days in advance.
Your line manager has 72 hours to approve or deny standard leave requests.

## 2. Sick Leave

Acme Corp separates sick leave from general PTO to ensure employees do not feel pressured to use vacation time when ill.

### 2.1 Allocation
Every full-time employee receives a flat allocation of 10 Sick Days on January 1st of each year. New hires joining after January 1st receive a prorated amount (e.g., joining on July 1st yields 5 days).
*   **Carryover:** Sick leave does not carry over. Balances reset to 10 on January 1st.
*   **Medical Certificates:** A doctor's note (Form AC-89) is strictly required if you take more than 3 consecutive sick days. This must be uploaded to the AcmeLeave Portal within 48 hours of your return to work.

## 3. Parental Leave

We support growing families through our comprehensive parental leave program.

### 3.1 Primary Caregiver Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid leave. This can be taken continuously or split into two 8-week blocks within the first 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.
*   **Phased Return:** Following the 16 weeks, primary caregivers may opt for a 4-week "phased return," working 50% hours at 100% pay.

### 3.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 6 months of the child's arrival.

## 4. Special Leave Categories

### 4.1 Bereavement Leave
*   **Immediate Family:** 5 fully paid days.
*   **Extended Family:** 3 fully paid days.
Immediate family is defined as a spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, or sibling. 

### 4.2 Sabbatical Leave
After completing 5 continuous years of full-time service, employees are eligible for a 4-week paid Sabbatical. 
*   **Condition:** Sabbaticals must be taken in a single, continuous 4-week block.
*   **Notice:** Sabbaticals require a minimum of 90 days' notice and approval from the Department VP.

## 5. Company Holidays

Acme Corp observes 11 standard company holidays annually across all North American offices. (International offices observe local statutory holidays).

1.  New Year's Day (January 1)
2.  Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
3.  President's Day (Third Monday in February)
4.  Acme Founder's Day (April 12th - *Company specific*)
5.  Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
6.  Juneteenth (June 19)
7.  Independence Day (July 4)
8.  Labor Day (First Monday in September)
9.  Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
10. Day after Thanksgiving 
11. Christmas Day (December 25)

If a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.
